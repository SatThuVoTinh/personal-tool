**Tiering criterion, stated so it is not silently a judgment call.** A tier is the risk **of the fix going wrong**,
not the severity of the defect:

* **`M12/T2.1` — high**: a wrong fix destroys or corrupts state, exposes secret or untrusted material, or fails in a
  way that cannot be detected afterwards. Each item must be reversible and must carry its own falsifiable probe.
* **`M12/T2.2` — medium**: a wrong fix changes behaviour or a gate, but the damage is visible — a red suite, a
  named refusal, a contradicted assertion. Includes every item needing a **required-gate change** (I1-A: those do
  not ride in the same wave as behaviour fixes).
* **`M12/T2.3` — low**: a wrong fix is a wording error in a document or a report field. Reversible by reading it.

An item is therefore in T2.1 even when the defect is Medium, and stays out of T2.1 even when the defect is Critical,
if the fix itself is safe. Severity is recorded, not used as authorization — the same rule R2's header stated.
